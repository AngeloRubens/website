Foojay is proud to be a media partner for two very different, but equally
great, events coming up: **OCX 2027** in Brussels and the fully online
**jChampions Conference 2027**.

## OCX 2027

We're excited to be a media partner of [Open Community Experience
(OCX) 2027](https://ocxconf.org/). From **13–15 April 2027**, developers,
maintainers, researchers, engineering leaders, and open source communities
from around the world will meet at **Tour & Taxis** in Brussels, Belgium.

* **Date:** April 13-15, 2027  
* **Location:** Tour & Taxis, Avenue du Port, Brussels, Belgium  
* **Details & tickets:** [ocxconf.org](https://ocxconf.org/)

### Call for Proposals

The Call for Proposals for OCX 2027 is now open. We'd like to invite our community to share their expertise at this
international conference. Head to [ocxconf.org](https://ocxconf.org/call-for-proposals/) for the
submission details.

## jChampions Conference 2027

This is already the **seventh edition** of the [jChampions
Conference](https://jchampionsconf.com/), the free, fully online conference
organized by and featuring Java Champions. It runs across four days — **January
21–22 and 25–26, 2027** — with seven sessions a day from **9:00 AM to 4:00 PM
EST**, live on StreamYard and streamed to YouTube.

* **Dates:** January 21-22 and 25-26, 2027  
* **Format:** Virtual, "Virtually Everywhere"  
* **Details:** [jchampionsconf.com](https://jchampionsconf.com/)

### Call for Proposals

The CFP is open now through
[Sessionize](https://sessionize.com/jchampions-conference-2027/):

* **Opens:** September 21, 2026
* **Closes:** October 26, 2026

Presentations are one hour, including time for audience questions, and can be
given in any language (submissions for committee review must be in English).
Speakers need to be a Java Champion, though a co-speaker without that
designation is welcome to join them on stage. You can suggest your preferred
time slot, and the organizers will try to accommodate it. Recordings of
previous editions are on the conference's YouTube channel.

Two great reasons to block your calendar for 2027. We hope to see you, in
person in Brussels or online with the Java Champions.
